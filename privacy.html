Quota Vault Privacy Policy

Effective October 24, 2026 · Applies to the Quota Vault app for Android

In short: Quota Vault does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal data. The app has no internet permission, no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Everything you enter stays on your phone, encrypted. We only ever receive what you choose to email us yourself: your feedback, or a crash report that contains none of your data.
We will NEVER ask for your data. Quota Vault and its developer will never ask for your personal information, your clients' information, your backup files or your passwords: not by email, phone, text or social media, and not even for support. The only things you might ever send us are your own feedback or a crash report, if you choose to, and we never need any of your data for either. Anyone who asks for your data or your clients' data is a scammer. Don't reply, don't share anything, and block them.

1. Who we are

Quota Vault ("the app") is a sales productivity app published on Google Play. In this policy, "we" means the app's developer.

2. Information we collect

None. We do not collect any information from or about you or your clients. The app does not have Android's internet permission, so it is technically unable to send data anywhere. It contains no analytics, advertising, third-party crash-reporting or tracking software, and no third-party services that could collect data.

Crash reports are completely optional and entirely up to you. The app never sends a crash report, or anything else, on its own. Sending one is always your choice, and you can turn crash reports off completely.

How crash reports work. If the app closes unexpectedly, it saves a short technical report on your phone only, unless you have turned this off in Settings → Security & privacy → Crash reports. The report contains the app version, the Android version, the phone's make and model, the time of the crash, and the type of error and the places in the app's code where it happened. Error messages are removed because they can contain text you typed. A report never contains your sales, clients, notes, settings or anything else you entered. The app asks whether you want to send it; if you do, your own email app opens with the report addressed to us, and you can read it and decide whether to send it. If you send it, we receive the report and the email address you send it from, and use them only to fix the problem and reply to you. Reports are deleted from your phone after 30 days, or sooner with Delete or Erase all data. You can view or delete them any time in Settings → Security & privacy → Crash reports, where you can also switch off saving them; switching it off deletes any saved reports. To have an email you sent us deleted, ask us at the address below.

Feedback. You can email us ideas, problems or questions any time from Settings → Send feedback. Your own email app opens with the message addressed to us, and nothing is sent until you press Send. We receive what you write and your email address, plus the app version, Android version and phone model only if you leave that box ticked (the app shows exactly what will be added). We use them only to read your message, reply and improve the app, and never share them. Please don't include client details. To have an email you sent us deleted, ask us at the address below.

3. Information you store in the app

You may enter information such as sales records, client names, phone numbers, email addresses, job or work order numbers, notes, appointments, tasks, goals, commission details, business expenses, mileage and photos of receipts. This information:

  • is stored only on your device, in the app's private storage;
  • is encrypted with AES-256 using a key held in your phone's secure hardware (Android Keystore), which cannot be copied off the device;
  • is never sent to us or anyone else, and we cannot see, access or recover it;
  • is excluded from Google's automatic cloud backup and device-to-device transfer.

You decide whether to create an encrypted backup file, a PDF report or a sales or expense spreadsheet (CSV), and where to save or send it. Backups are always protected by a password you choose; PDF reports can be. Spreadsheet files cannot be password-protected, so the app tells you so before you create one. Reports never include client names, phone numbers, emails or notes: they show each sale's date and job or work order number instead, so sales can be verified (you can turn the numbers off), and your commission only if you turn it on. Once you save or share a file outside the app, how it is handled depends on the place you put it (for example, your cloud storage or email provider).

The backup folder

Backups are saved to one folder on your phone, Documents › Quota Vault Backups, the same place every time, so you always know where they are and restoring finds them by itself. The folder is made when you first set up the app and stays if the app is deleted. No storage permission is used: on Android 10 and later the app can save files to that folder and see only the files it saved itself. After reinstalling the app or on a new phone, it can see earlier backups only if you allow that one folder in Android's own folder picker (on Android 8 and 9 you choose the folder this way the first time you back up). The app can't see anything else on your phone. Every backup in the folder is encrypted with your password, so it is unreadable to anyone without it. The folder does not survive the phone being lost or reset, so the app also offers to save a copy anywhere you choose, such as Google Drive or a computer.

Receipt photos

You can attach photos of receipts to expenses. The app does not ask for permission to use your camera, photos or files. A photo reaches the app only when you take one with your phone's camera app or pick one in Android's photo picker, and the app receives that one photo and nothing else. Before it is stored, the photo is shrunk and re-saved without its hidden details (such as GPS location and phone model), then encrypted like the rest of your data. Opening a receipt asks for your phone's PIN, password, pattern or fingerprint every time, unless you turn that off (turning it off asks too). The viewer blocks screenshots. Receipt photos are included in backup files, inside the same password encryption as everything else, so restoring a backup brings them back. You can also save a copy of any receipt to a place you choose.

Client information stays inside the app

  • Screenshots, screen recording, screen sharing and the recent-apps preview are blocked by default, and on-screen assistants see a blank window. You can allow screenshots in Security & privacy (it asks for your phone's lock first); receipt photos stay protected either way.
  • Nothing can be copied or cut out of the app, so client details never reach the clipboard.
  • Your keyboard is asked not to learn from anything you type in the app (the same request incognito browser tabs make).
  • Autofill services and Android's on-device "content capture" are switched off for the app.
  • Reminder notifications never show an appointment or task title, a client's name or notes, only when it's due, because notifications can be read by other apps, watches, cars and phone-to-computer links.
  • Reports and spreadsheets never contain client names, phone numbers, emails or notes.
  • The only file that can hold client details is a backup, which is always encrypted with a password you choose and is meant only for restoring into Quota Vault.
  • Tapping Call or Email on a client hands that one number or address to your phone or email app, because that's how the call or email is made.

No app can stop someone from photographing the screen with another device, or protect a phone that has been rooted or infected with malware.

4. Permissions the app uses

PermissionWhy
NotificationsShows the reminders you set. A reminder says only that an appointment or task is due and when; never its title, the client or notes.
Alarms & reminders (exact alarms)Makes reminders ring on time. You can turn this off in your phone's settings.
Run at startupRe-sets your reminders after the phone restarts.
Biometrics (fingerprint / face)Only if you turn on app lock. Android performs the check; the app never receives your fingerprint, face data or PIN.

Automatic backups (optional, off until you turn them on): the app saves the same password-encrypted backup file to your backup folder on its own, daily, weekly or monthly, and keeps the latest 5. To do that without asking, it keeps your backup password sealed with this phone's Android Keystore key, which can't be copied off the phone. Turning automatic backups off, or erasing everything, wipes that saved password. Nothing is sent anywhere.

Importing a spreadsheet: Settings → Import from a spreadsheet opens Android's file picker. The app can read only the one CSV file you choose, once, to add its clients or sales. It keeps no access to that file or any other, and nothing is sent anywhere.

The app never requests access to the internet, contacts, location, camera, microphone, photos, files, phone calls or SMS. Receipt photos come only from the camera app or the photo picker, one photo at a time, as described above. Backups use the backup folder described above, with no storage permission.

5. Sharing and selling

We do not share, sell, rent or trade any data. Crash reports you email us are never shared with anyone.

6. Google Play, ratings and reviews

When you download or buy the app, Google processes that transaction under Google's Privacy Policy. We receive only the aggregated, non-identifying statistics that Google Play provides to all developers (such as install counts) and, for purchases, the order details Google provides to sellers.

Ratings and reviews. Settings → Rate Quota Vault opens the app's page in the Google Play Store. Now and then, after you log a sale (no sooner than a week after you start using the app, and at most a few times a year), the app may also ask Google Play to show its in-app rating window. That window belongs to the Play Store app, which handles any rating or review you leave under Google's Privacy Policy. Quota Vault sends nothing, asks you no questions of its own first, and never learns whether or how you rated it. Google Play limits how often the window can appear, so it may not appear at all. You can turn this off in Settings → Rate Quota Vault.

7. Data retention and deletion

Your data stays on your device until you delete it. You can delete individual records, or use Settings → Security & privacy → Erase all data, which permanently removes everything and destroys the encryption key. Uninstalling the app also deletes all app data from the device. Backups in Documents › Quota Vault Backups are kept (so you can restore them later) unless you delete them: in the app, tap the trash icon next to any backup in Security & privacy, or tick Also delete my backups when erasing. Copies you saved elsewhere are under your control and must be deleted by you.

8. Security

The app offers encryption at rest, an optional fingerprint/face/PIN app lock, screenshot blocking, privacy mode and password-protected backups and reports. No method of storage is perfectly secure, so please keep your phone's screen lock on, keep its software up to date, and keep your backup passwords safe. We cannot reset or recover a forgotten password.

If you add the app's home-screen widgets, they show your tasks, appointments and sales counts on your home screen, where anyone holding the phone can see them. They never show a client's full name, phone or email: the Follow-ups widget shows only a job number or first name. They show money amounts (your projected earnings and commission) only if you allow it. The Quick add widget shows no information at all, and the app's lock still asks first when it is on. You can hide all widget contents in Settings → Security & privacy.

9. Children

The app is intended for business use by adults and is not directed at children under 13 (or the equivalent age in your country).

10. Your rights

Because we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct, export or delete on your behalf. You can view, change, export (via encrypted backup) and delete all of your data directly in the app. If you store information about your clients, you are responsible for meeting the privacy laws that apply to you.

11. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date. If a change affects how the app handles data, the app will ask you to review it before you continue.
