Join the Quota Vault beta

Help test the private sales tracker before it launches on Google Play. It takes about two minutes, and it's free while you test.

You need an Android phone and the Google account you use for the Play Store.

  1. Join the tester group

    Sign in with your Play Store Google account, then tap Join group.

    Join the group

  2. Become a tester

    Tap Become a tester. If it says the app isn't available, wait a few minutes after joining the group and try again.

    Become a tester

  3. Install Quota Vault

    Install it from Google Play like any other app.

    Get it on Google Play

  4. Keep it for 14 days

    Google needs testers to stay in for two weeks before the app can launch. Open it a few times, log a sale or two, and say what you think. You can leave private feedback on the app's Play Store page, and only the developer sees it.

Joining the group shares your Google account email with the developer, because that's how Google lets testers in. The app itself has no internet access and never sees it.
